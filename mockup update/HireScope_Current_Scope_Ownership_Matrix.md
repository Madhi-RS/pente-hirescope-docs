# HireScope Current Scope — Module, Workflow & Ownership Matrix

**Prepared:** 25 November 2026  
**Scope decision:** This matrix is for one operational HireScope workspace only: **Pente**. It covers the roadmap’s current recruitment lifecycle (01–13), the requested offer/onboarding/position-close extension (14–16), and the narrow employee-record capability shown in `hirescope-design.html`. Multi-business provisioning, cross-organisation switching, and Organisation Owner appointment/replacement are out of this delivery scope: Pente has one fixed Organisation Owner and one fixed Organisation Admin. It does **not** add the future HRMS domains on the roadmap: time and attendance, leave, payroll, performance, mobility, or offboarding.

## How to use this file

Each deliverable is a tab-separated table inside a code block. Copy the contents of one block and paste it into cell A1 of a Google Sheet; Sheets will distribute the fields into columns. Rows are intentionally atomic so the Master sheet can be filtered by Phase, Module, or Primary Owner.

## Evidence and ownership rules used

- **Current functional boundary:** `HRMS Roadmap.png` (current scope 01–13; external handoff 14–16; HRMS A–I is future scope).
- **User-flow reference:** `hirescope-design.html`, including HR Offer Management, Employee Onboarding, Employee Directory, and Employee Profile screens. Its offer/onboarding/employee interactions are browser-mockup behavior, not proof of backend implementation.
- **Implementation truth:** `IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md` (24 Nov 2026): Steward PKCE session and organisation-role recognition exist; recruitment workflow APIs and the 55-table domain catalogue are not implemented.
- **Architecture/data traceability:** `System_Architecture.md` and `mockup update/pente_db_diagram.dbml`, especially `application_selections`, `onboarding_records`, `position_fills`, `notification_outbox`, `audit_events`, and the applicant/interview tables.
- **Steward capability boundary:** `SDK_FEATURES.md`. Only documented operations are named below. A blank/no capability means the capability is not evidenced in the supplied Steward inventory; it is a gap, not an assumed integration.
- **Employee-record boundary:** HireScope may maintain the limited employment profile created after completed onboarding (identity/contact, employment, department, manager, joining date, location, status, documents, history) because the current mockup requires it. Steward can supply the people/unit/reporting-line directory, but the supplied SDK does **not** evidence a create/update employee-record API. This is not a full HRIS source-of-truth claim.
- **Single-workspace operating boundary:** The Organisation Owner configures Pente-wide recruitment defaults and guardrails. The Organisation Admin maintains Pente members and their recruitment access. Hiring Managers configure a requisition/opening only within Owner-approved defaults; HR makes all candidate progression/selection decisions. Audio assessment is configurable where approved; video assessment is **not planned in this scope**.
- **Withdrawal and retest rule:** If an invited applicant does not attend/submit by the configured deadline and does not submit an in-product grievance within 24 hours, the application is treated as withdrawn. HR may subsequently decide whether a six-month retest eligibility date is justified from the grievance or other recorded evidence (for example, a phone call or email); the original application never reopens.
- **Background-verification rule:** A conditional offer may trigger a third-party **SpringVerify** background/reference verification case. HireScope must collect/record the permitted consent, request and outcome; provider result is advisory to HR and never automatically rejects or selects an applicant.

Ownership key: **Steward** = a documented Steward capability is the source of truth; **HireScope** = recruitment/product logic and its records; **Hybrid** = both systems have distinct, non-duplicated responsibilities.

## Explicit exclusions

The following roadmap items remain future HRMS scope and must not be pulled into current HireScope merely because an employee directory exists: multi-business/workspace provisioning, cross-organisation switching, Organisation Owner appointment/replacement, payroll/compensation administration, time and attendance, shifts, leave/holidays, performance management, learning/career, employee communication/self-service beyond recruitment/onboarding notices, internal mobility, offboarding, alumni, and rehire management. A future HRMS source-of-truth decision may later replace or constrain the limited employee-record capability in this matrix.
